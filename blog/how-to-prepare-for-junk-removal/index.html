Do I need to move items outside before junk removal?

Not at all. That's what you're hiring us for. Items can be inside, upstairs, in the attic, or behind the shed we do all the carrying and loading. Just point us at what's going.

What should I do to prepare for a junk removal pickup?

Call, email, or send the quote form. A photo or two of the pile helps us price it accurately.

Can I be absent during junk removal pickup?

For most jobs it helps to have someone confirm what's going, but it isn't always required. For curbside piles or clearly marked items, we can often handle it with a phone confirmation and photos.

What happens when the junk removal crew arrives?

We arrive in your window, call ahead, and do every bit of the lifting, carrying, and loading.
