What it costs

Junk removal is priced by volume — how much room your load takes up in the truck. A couple of items costs a fraction of a full load, and you get the number before we pick anything up.

Good to know: photos speed things up enormously. Text or email a picture of the pile and we can usually quote before we even arrive.

Handled with care

Cleanouts often come at hard moments — a loss in the family, a foreclosure, a move nobody planned. We keep the process calm: we work room by room, set aside anything you flag, and never rush you through decisions.

Good to know

What we can and can't haul

If an item isn't listed here, just ask. We'll give you a straight answer and point you elsewhere if we can't help.

We happily take

  • Furniture, mattresses, and household goods
  • Appliances and electronics
  • Yard waste, brush, and limbs
  • Construction and remodel debris
  • Scrap metal and old fencing
  • Boxes, bags, and general clutter
  • Hot tubs, sheds, and play equipment

We can't take

  • Wet paint, solvents, and chemicals
  • Motor oil, fuel, and antifreeze
  • Asbestos and hazardous building materials
  • Medical and biological waste
  • Ammunition and explosives
  • Anything requiring a hazmat permit to transport
