FAQ

Before you call

A few quick answers to the questions that come up most.

How much does junk removal cost?+

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in the truck, not by the hour. A few boxes cost far less than a full load, and you always get a firm price on site before we load anything.

How soon can you come out?+

We hold room in the schedule for same-day and next-day pickups, Monday through Saturday. Call early in the day for the best chance of a same-day slot.

Do I need to move anything outside first?+

No. Items can be inside, upstairs, in the attic, or behind the shed. We do all the carrying and loading — just show us what's going.

What information helps you quote faster?+

A photo or two of the pile, a rough idea of the volume, and any access details — stairs, narrow hallways, gate width, or how close we can park. With those we can often quote before arriving.

What can't you haul?+

No hazardous materials — wet paint, solvents, motor oil, chemicals, asbestos, or medical waste. If you're unsure, call and describe the item; if we can't take it we'll tell you who can.

Do you charge for the quote?+

Never. Quotes are free with no obligation, whether over the phone, by email, or on site.
