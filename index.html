FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Still not sure about something? Call (940) 372-1737 — we're happy to talk it through.

How much does junk removal cost?+

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in the truck, not by the hour — so a few boxes cost far less than a full load. We give you a firm price on site before we load anything, and quotes are always free.

Do I need to move everything outside first?+

Not at all. That's what you're hiring us for. Items can be inside, upstairs, in the attic, or behind the shed — we do all the carrying and loading. Just point us at what's going.

How soon can you come out?+

We keep room in the schedule for same-day and next-day pickups, and we work Monday through Saturday. Call early in the day for the best chance at a same-day slot.

What can't you take?+

We can't haul hazardous materials — things like wet paint, solvents, motor oil, asbestos, chemicals, or medical waste. If you're unsure about an item, call and describe it. If we can't take it, we'll point you to who can.

What happens to the stuff you haul away?+

Anything still usable goes to donation, and metals and recyclables get separated out. The landfill is our last resort, not our first stop.

Do I have to be there for the pickup?+

For most jobs it helps to have someone confirm what's going, but it isn't always required. For curbside piles or clearly marked items, we can often handle it with a phone confirmation and photos.

How do I pay?+

Payment is due once the job is finished and you're happy with the result — never up front. Call us to confirm the payment methods we currently accept.

Do you handle big single items like a hot tub or a shed?+

Yes. Large single items and light demolition — hot tubs, sheds, swing sets, above-ground pools — are jobs we take on regularly. Send a photo and we'll quote it.
